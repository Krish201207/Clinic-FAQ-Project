What are the clinic's opening hours?
The clinic is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Is the clinic open on public holidays?
No. The clinic is closed on public holidays. A holiday schedule is posted at reception and on the notice board two weeks in advance.

What time does the clinic stop accepting walk-ins?
Walk-in registration closes 30 minutes before closing time, at 5:30 PM on weekdays and 12:30 PM on Saturdays.

Where is the clinic located?
The clinic is at 12 Market Road, next to the Community Hall, opposite the main bus stand.

Is parking available at the clinic?
Yes. Free parking for about 20 two-wheelers and 8 cars is available behind the building. Reserved spaces are provided near the ramp for patients with disabilities.

Is the clinic wheelchair accessible?
Yes. The clinic has a ground-floor entrance with a ramp, wide doors, an accessible restroom and wheelchairs available at reception.

What is the clinic's phone number?
You can call reception on 0181-555-0142 during opening hours. Calls outside these hours go to voicemail and are returned the next working day.

Can I contact the clinic by email?
Yes, at info@greenfieldclinic.example. Emails are answered within 2 working days. Do not send urgent medical concerns by email.

How do I book an appointment?
You can book by phone, at reception, or through the clinic's online booking form. You will need your name, phone number and preferred date and time.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?
Walk-ins are accepted for general consultations, but appointments are seen first. Waiting times for walk-ins may be longer.

How do I cancel or reschedule my appointment?
Please call reception or use the online form at least 4 hours before your appointment so the slot can be offered to another patient.

What happens if I am late for my appointment?
If you are more than 15 minutes late, your appointment may be rescheduled or you may be seen after other patients, depending on the schedule.

How far in advance can I book?
Appointments can be booked up to 30 days in advance.

How long is a standard consultation?
A standard consultation is about 15 to 20 minutes. Follow-up and counselling visits may be up to 30 minutes.

How much is a general consultation?
A general consultation costs Rs. 100. Consultations are free for patients holding a valid community health card.

What payment methods are accepted?
The clinic accepts cash, UPI and debit/credit cards. Receipts are provided for all payments.

Do you offer free or discounted services?
Yes. Community health card holders, senior citizens above 65 and children under 5 receive free or discounted consultations. Ask reception for eligibility details.

What is the fee for a blood sugar test?
A fasting or random blood sugar test costs Rs. 40.

What services does the clinic provide?
Services include general consultations, child immunization, antenatal check-ups, basic lab tests, blood pressure and diabetes screening, wound dressing, and health counselling.

Does the clinic offer vaccinations?
Yes. Routine child immunizations follow the national schedule and are given on Tuesdays and Thursdays, 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM. Please bring the child's immunization card.

Do you provide antenatal check-ups?
Yes. Antenatal clinics run on Wednesdays, 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM, by appointment. Please bring your antenatal card and previous reports.

Is there a pharmacy at the clinic?
Yes. A small dispensary supplies common prescribed medicines at subsidized rates. Medicines not in stock can be bought at nearby pharmacies.

Which lab tests can be done at the clinic?
Basic tests include blood sugar, hemoglobin, urine routine, pregnancy test and malaria rapid test. Other tests are sent to a partner laboratory.

Do I need to fast before a blood sugar test?
For a fasting blood sugar test, do not eat for 8 hours before the test; water is allowed. A random blood sugar test needs no fasting.

How long do lab results take?
Basic in-clinic tests are ready the same day. Tests sent to the partner laboratory usually take 2 to 3 working days.

How can I collect my lab report?
Reports can be collected from reception with your patient ID or token slip. Results are not given over the phone.

What documents do I need for first-time registration?
Please bring a photo ID, your phone number and address, and your community health card if you have one. Registration takes about 5 to 10 minutes.

How do I update my phone number or address?
Tell reception at your next visit or call the clinic with your patient ID and a photo ID check at the front desk.

Can I register my children or family members?
Yes. A parent or guardian can register children. Bring each child's birth certificate or immunization card and the guardian's photo ID.

How do I request a copy of my medical records?
Submit a written request at reception with a photo ID. Copies are usually ready within 5 working days. A small copying charge may apply.

Can a family member collect my records for me?
Only with your signed written consent and a photo ID of both the patient and the person collecting.

Is my personal information kept confidential?
Yes. Patient information is stored securely and shared only with the care team or when required by law. See the clinic's Privacy Notice at reception.

Can I bring someone with me to my appointment?
Yes. One companion may accompany you. Children and patients needing support may be accompanied by a guardian or caregiver.

What should I bring to my appointment?
Bring your photo ID, patient ID or token, any previous prescriptions or reports, and a list of medicines you currently take.

Which languages do clinic staff speak?
Staff can assist in English, Hindi and Punjabi. Please ask reception if you need interpretation support.

Do I need to wear a mask in the clinic?
Masks are recommended for patients with cough, cold or fever, and are available free at reception.

How can I give feedback or make a complaint?
Use the feedback box at reception, email info@greenfieldclinic.example, or ask to speak with the clinic manager. Complaints are acknowledged within 3 working days.

Does the clinic offer home visits?
No. Home visits are not provided. Community health workers may run periodic outreach camps; dates are posted at the clinic.

How does a referral to a hospital work?
If the doctor decides you need specialist care, the clinic prepares a referral letter and tells you which partner hospital to visit. Ask reception about transport support options.

The clinic holds a free health screening camp on the first Saturday of every month. Details are on the notice board and at reception.
